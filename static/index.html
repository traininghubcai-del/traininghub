Logistics & FAQ

Is there a cost to attend?

No — training is provided at no cost to active M&A Supply dealers.

What should I bring?

Bring your own hand tools and PPE. Training materials are provided on-site.

Is lunch and parking provided?

Lunch is provided. Free parking is available at the branch.

Who do I call if I have a problem?

Call your local M&A branch — they handle seats, directions and anything on the day. Your Territory Manager can help with your account.

What if I need to cancel?

Let your M&A branch know at least 48 hours ahead so we can offer the seat to another dealer.
